That's the tutorial. You've seen components and their state, parameters and events,
bindings and styles, content and services, and then routing, forms, auth, a server and
tests. Everything you'd use in a real app.

When you need the details, three places help:

- The [language reference](https://github.com/HolgerTheHuck/Tsazor/blob/main/docs/language-reference.md)
  covers every directive, attribute and built-in component, with the exact rules.
- [@tsazor/fluent](https://github.com/HolgerTheHuck/Tsazor/blob/main/docs/fluent.md) is a
  component library in the Fluent 2 design: buttons, dialogs, data grids and more. You can
  install it, or copy a component's source into your project and make it your own.
- The [design principles](https://github.com/HolgerTheHuck/Tsazor/blob/main/docs/design-principles.md)
  explain the choices Tsazor makes, like strict types everywhere, and why.

And the best next step is the one this tutorial kept asking for: build something small and
change it until you know how it reacts. Happy baking.
