That's the tutorial: the Blazor model you know, in TypeScript, from components to routing,
forms, auth, a typed backend client and tests.

For the details:

- The [language reference](https://github.com/HolgerTheHuck/Tsazor/blob/main/docs/language-reference.md)
  lists every directive and component, and where Tsazor deviates from Blazor.
- [@tsazor/fluent](https://github.com/HolgerTheHuck/Tsazor/blob/main/docs/fluent.md) is the
  component library, in Microsoft's Fluent 2 design and written in `.tsazor`. You can also
  copy a component's source into your project.
- The [design principles](https://github.com/HolgerTheHuck/Tsazor/blob/main/docs/design-principles.md)
  explain what stays like Blazor, what deliberately doesn't, and why.

Start with one page of your app: port it, run `tsazor check`, write a test for it. Happy
baking.
